# Disclaimer

Read this before using anything in this repository on a live procurement.

## What these tools are

Structured aids for classification, cost modelling, and gap identification. They help a procurement professional prepare, analyze, and ask better questions.

## What these tools are not

They are not legal advice, and they are not a substitute for it. Several tools in this repository touch contract terms, indemnities, privacy obligations, and public sector governance requirements. Those areas need qualified review, and nothing here replaces it.

They are not approvals. A routing result is not a cleared purchase. A gap analysis is not a reviewed contract. A cost model is not a business case.

They are not a compliance assessment. Where a tool references a directive, a statute, or a regulator's guidance, it does so to indicate what may be triggered. Confirming what actually applies to your organization is your responsibility.

## Accuracy and currency

AI pricing, model lineups, and vendor terms change quickly. Any rate, figure, or market observation in this repository is a snapshot with a stated date and may be out of date by the time you read it. Verify against vendor documentation before any number enters a business case or a negotiation position.

Where these tools describe common vendor positions or typical contract language, those are general observations. Your vendor's actual paper is what governs.

## Outputs need review

These tools are run in AI assistants, and AI assistants produce confident output that is sometimes wrong. Every result needs a human to check it against the source material before it is relied on or shared. This is true of the tools in this repository exactly as it is true of any other AI output.

Do not forward a tool output to a decision maker without reviewing it and putting your name on it.

## Confidential information

Do not upload confidential, commercially sensitive, supplier identifying, or personal information into an AI service unless your organization permits it and that specific service is approved for that class of information.

For public sector users, this includes information governed by access and privacy legislation. Check your organization's position before pasting anything from a live procurement.

Remove names, values, and identifying detail before running any tool here on real material. The tools are designed to work on anonymized input.
